# NBA Training Camps Begin: Bobby Marks Examines 30 Teams

As training camps get underway across the league, ESPN’s Bobby Marks provides an in-depth look at the roster, contract, and fit questions for all 30 NBA teams. This comprehensive guide aims to address key issues and potential storylines for each team as they prepare for the upcoming season.

## Why it matters
The insights from Bobby Marks are crucial for fans, analysts, and team management as they offer a preview of the challenges and opportunities each team faces. By examining the current state of each roster and the dynamics within, Marks helps to set expectations for the season ahead, making it easier for stakeholders to follow and engage with the evolving narratives of the NBA.
