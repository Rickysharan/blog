The Apothecary Diaries is getting a new anime film scheduled to open in Japanese theaters on December 11. The project features a brand-new story by Hyuganatsu, author of the original novel series. Mariya Ise is joining the cast as Mu Qing, although the English romanization of the character's name has not been confirmed.

The story follows Maomao and Jinshi as they travel to the homeland of a concubine who died five years earlier in the imperial harem. Their task is to return her remains, but the journey brings them into a story involving pirates, hidden treasure, the secret surrounding the Empress' remains, and a mysterious boy.

## Why it matters

The film adds a new story from the original author instead of simply repeating an existing television episode. Its December release also falls between the two parts of the anime's third season: the first began on October 2, while the second is scheduled for April 2027. That timetable gives viewers a separate film story during the television season's break.
