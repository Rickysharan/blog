During a season that boasted the long-awaited return of Magical Girl Lyrical Nanoha after an eleven-year absence from our screens, and with an unexpected revival of Magical Princess Minky Momo (after 32 years!) announced as an upcoming fall 2026 OVA, the second season of Magilumiere Magical Girls Inc. seemed to be overshadowed. Streaming on Amazon Prime Video, a platform not typically frequented for anime, and with pacing issues in the first two story arcs, the show was lost among the retro magical girl clamor. This is a real shame, as it's a clever, entertaining, optimistic show that deserves an audience. The final story arc, however, is head and shoulders above the rest, making the show worth sticking with. ## Why it matters Magilumiere's second season highlights the importance of proper streaming platforms and pacing in ensuring a show reaches its intended audience and fully realizes its potential.
