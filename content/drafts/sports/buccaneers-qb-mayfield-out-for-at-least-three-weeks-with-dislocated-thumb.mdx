Baker Mayfield is expected to miss at least three weeks after dislocating the thumb on his throwing hand, according to an ESPN report. Buccaneers coach Todd Bowles announced the expected absence on Monday after the quarterback suffered the injury on Sunday.

The report gives a minimum expected absence rather than a confirmed return date. The source summary does not specify a replacement quarterback, a treatment plan or a date for a further update.

## Why it matters

A quarterback's absence is relevant to how readers follow the team's upcoming games, but the information available here is limited. The reported three-week estimate should not be read as a guarantee that Mayfield will return immediately after that period. Further reporting would be needed to explain the team's plans and his recovery in more detail.
