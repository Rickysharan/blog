## Why it matters
The preseason is crucial for teams to assess their strengths and weaknesses, and these questions highlight key areas of focus. For fans and analysts, these questions provide insight into the upcoming season's potential storylines and competitive landscape. By addressing these issues, teams can better prepare for the regular season and beyond, impacting their performance and the overall league dynamics.
